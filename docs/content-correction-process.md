# Content correction and update process

1. Identify the page, statement or source that may be inaccurate, outdated, harmful or unclear.
2. Verify the concern against authoritative sources, preferably primary public-health sources.
3. If a concern may affect safety, prioritise review and temporarily remove or clearly qualify the claim where appropriate.
4. Record the original wording, correction, reason, sources, editor and date in the repository change or internal review record.
5. Update the content and its references. Never imply clinical review has occurred unless it has.
6. Run the build, audit and browser tests.
7. Publish a correction note on the page when a substantive correction changes the meaning or practical takeaway.
8. Recheck the source links and date fields.

## Reader reports

Until a monitored contact channel is approved, the project should not claim that readers can submit corrections through a contact form. Once a contact channel is available, document it here and ensure someone is responsible for monitoring it.
